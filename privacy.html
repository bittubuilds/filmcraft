FilmCraft Android App — Privacy Policy

This app is a viewer for the open-source FilmCraft video editor (https://getartcraft.com/apps/filmcraft, MIT/Apache-2.0 licensed). The editor itself runs inside the app using WebAssembly.

  • Internet access: the app loads the editor from https://bittubuilds.github.io/filmcraft/. An internet connection is required on first launch.
  • No data collection: the app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.
  • Your media: videos, images and audio you import stay on your device. Exports are saved to your device's Downloads folder. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • Permissions: the app only requests the INTERNET permission. Access to your media files happens only through the system file picker when you choose to import, and storage saving happens only for your own exports.

Questions: contact via https://github.com/bittubuilds/filmcraft
